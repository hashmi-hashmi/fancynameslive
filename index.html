Privacy Policy — Fancy Names Live

Effective date: 2 November 2026
Developer: Tales in Rain

1. What this app is

Fancy Names Live is an offline studio for live streamers. It writes names and quotes on screen with a glowing quill, and lets streamers style their live scene with themes, desks, foregrounds and music.

2. Data we collect: none

The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no tracking. Everything you create or upload — names, stories, themes, images, music — stays on your own device.

3. Permissions

The app may ask for access to photos/media on your device only so you can upload your own backgrounds, desks, foregrounds or music, and save your photos and videos. Media you pick never leaves your device.

4. In-app purchases

The app may offer optional subscription plans (Basic, Pro, Studio). Payments, if any, are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, see or store your payment details.

5. Children

The app is a general-audience creativity tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
